Hate

Currently listening to: [Justify / Good Ole Dayz | Horsepower Productions](https://horsepowerproductionstempa.bandcamp.com/album/justify-good-ole-dayz)

  What is contained in an insult? A self-deprecation of derision, the absence of value spit upon your own face as if straight from the camel's mouth. It's days like today that I'm reminded of how much I hate myself. 

I'll try to paint the picture as best I can - keep in mind I recently bombed at simply describing an odd apple and a weird tree. My recent job laid me off 3 weeks prior to the season ending, the bastards. So I hopped back onto the job application grind & stumbled onto a canvassing position. Of course the "interview" was anything but complicated, it operated more as an elongated onboarding session. No real qualifications needed & it pays $24 an hour. Probably the highest hourly rate I've ever been paid. Going into my first day I was super confident, up until the point where I arrived at the apartment complex I'd been assigned. 

My mind jammed up, the same as it ever does, when it comes time to follow through. Immediately I begin scheming to defraud the canvassing app by spoofing my location. I came upon an app that not only allowed me to spoof my location, it also included a JOYSTICK to move my location around as if I were walking, jogging, or sprinting. Drunk with power I blazed through my list, cycling through apartment numbers like clockwork every three to four minutes - sometimes up to eight minutes if I had to "walk" a further distance.

Midway through the day the guilt began to crawl through my toes, only ever increasing as the day progressed. Me and my wife are barely getting by! The peculiar type of financial stress that gnaws away at your psyche as you apply for your 15th job *that day.* When every new day is a new awakening of just how fucking useless your "jack of all trades" approach has gotten you - polymath my ass! Yet here I am, spoofing my way through a job as simple and basic as *talking to people* for ~6 hours of my day because I somehow cannot overcome myself and must cheat the system to win.

Let me explain - it's as if my mind cannot compel itself to simply follow through. Like there is a 1000 meter wall between me and "doing the task." Some days are obviously better than others, but the case remains. My ADHD diagnosis has partially helped me overcome this hurdle, but the non-stimulant prescription hasn't quite done the trick. So I continue to run away from simple things! It's as if I'm discovering, at the age of 36, that I am seemingly frightened to merely engage with others in certain situations. I've discovered that I need to warm up to any persons before more of my personality reveals itself.

At my last job I clocked this timeline at two to three weeks. Off the bat I hit things off great with my boss, but I knew earning the respect of my subordinates would take the longest amount of time. Coupled with the almost arguably default standard racial tension that comes in to play in Alabama - I knew this would be quite the challenge. And I love challenges! But those of you from states with centuries of systemic racism and oppression are sure to be familiar with the vibe. I'm proud to know I earned the respect of almost every black employee I worked with while there. Can't wait to roll a fatty and listen to some fantastic music with a few of them. The job was hard enough for me & I'm *unfortunately-fortunate* the same color as the entirety of upper management there. I don't think I've ever worked in a more racist environment. The kitchen at times would be as hot as 130F! It took months for management to provide a pathetic mobile air conditioner, and by size alone could not cool a room larger than 500 square feet, let alone the 1500 square feet the industrial kitchen was! And in the top few highest performing locations in the entire national company. For shame.

I know a partial reason for my perceived fear to "reveal" myself to others is due to some trauma related to my bipolar diagnosis
