In an effort to practice prose, I'm going to discuss what I'd like to assume is an entry-level prompt in a random community college level Creative Writing 101 class. The apple and the tree - write about them, any way you like, in any form you choose. Where would your head take you? Perhaps a literal interpretation, describing the color of the apple & the density of the leaves on the tree? How about the connection between the two, are they related; of the same soil? Is the apple a color we've become conditioned to? It might be a rich, deep purple, with light gradient ripples of white crawling up its side, interweaving to form lattices slowly scaling upwards. The tree that bore this fruit could have sparse branches curling in on themselves much like an overgrown toenail curves back towards the toe from which it sprouted. It towers over all of the other trees in the world in which it exists. The few leaves littering the cursed branches are long and bloated in a way that suggests they are infested with blight.

Maybe the prompt illicits a more religious interpretation, where the apple serves as a reminder of the allegory of the serpent in the Garden of Eden?
